Preschool Stories & More
Sep 28, 2026, 10:15 AM – 11:15 AM

Join us Monday mornings for an engaging and interactive story time for babies, toddlers, and preschoolers. Sep 29
Literary Collective
Sep 29, 2026, 7:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Join a community of readers for quarterly gatherings to discuss everything books and reading. Our autumn get-together will feature a book tasting, refreshments and brief discussion about what we’re all reading. No books will be assigned and challenges are optional. This no-pressure literary event is for adults who love reading. Registration is required; call the library to sign up.

Sep 30
Early-Out Hangout
Sep 30, 2026, 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM

Are you looking for activities on early out days? Stop by the library for Early Out Hangout. Find our usual activities like crafts and coloring, plus some extra things like Bingo, Bee Bots, and race tracks. Please note: Children must be 8 years old to be in the library without direct caregiver/parent supervision. Caregivers must be at least 12 years old.
